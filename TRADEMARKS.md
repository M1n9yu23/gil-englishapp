## Forks

You are welcome to fork the project, study it and build on it. If you publish a build of a fork,
on an app store or anywhere else:

- give it a different name and a different icon,
- use a different application ID,
- do not suggest that it is the original app or endorsed by this project.

You may say that your app is based on Gil, with a link to this repository.
