# Privacy policy

[한국어](PRIVACY.ko.md)

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy covers the Gil app for Android (application ID `com.gyugle.gil`).

Gil does not collect, share or sell personal data. There is no account and no server, and the app
does not request the internet permission.

## What the app keeps on your phone

- Learning progress: finished lessons, review schedule and results, mistakes, saved words and
  daily study time.
- Settings: daily goal, reminder times, theme, accent and speed, the Hangul sound guide and
  whether you finished the introduction.
- A lesson you left halfway, so that you can continue it later.
- For listening mode: its speed and pause, where you stopped, and the speech it plays, saved as
  audio files in the app's cache, which the system may clear when storage runs low.

You can erase your learning progress in the app's settings. Clearing the app's storage in the
system settings, or uninstalling the app, deletes all of it.

## Microphone

Speaking steps ask for the microphone permission so that you can record yourself and listen back.
A recording is at most 10 seconds long, stays in memory, and is never written to storage. It is
gone when you move to the next step or leave the lesson. On Android 13 or later, if the phone has
offline English speech recognition installed, the recording is also passed to that recognizer on
the phone to show which words it heard. The app asks only for on-device recognition. Without the
permission, the rest of the app works as usual.

## Speech, reminders and the widget

The app reads English aloud with the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone and passes it
only the text of the lesson. Listening mode has the same engine read the course's words, meanings
and sentences into audio files, and shows a media notification while it plays. Reminders are
scheduled on the phone and shown as notifications. If you add the home screen widget, it shows
how many reviews are due today and an example sentence for a word you have studied.

## Backup

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include your progress and
settings in that backup and restore them on a new phone. Google runs this backup under its own
terms, and the developer has no access to it. You can turn it off in the system settings.

In the app's settings you can also save your progress, goal, reminder times and settings to a file
you choose and restore them later. The app reads and writes only that file and never sends it out.

## Reporting a content error

"Report a content error" opens a GitHub form in your browser, filled in with where you saw the
error, the app version and the text on the screen. Nothing is sent unless you submit the form
yourself. What you submit is public on GitHub and covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

## Changes and contact

When this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date. For questions, use
the developer email on the app's Google Play page.
